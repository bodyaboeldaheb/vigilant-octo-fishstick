About Me

Student, builder, and someone who doesn't like stopping at a dream

Who I Am

I'm Abdo, from Egypt. I'm a student, but I don't see my life as only about school. I'm interested in technology, programming, business, science, research, and football — and in building a future that's actually mine. I'm ambitious and curious, and I usually don't like giving up on something that matters to me. If one path doesn't work, I look for another one instead of stopping completely.

Practical Experience

I've spent around three years working in my family's heavy-truck spare-parts and engine business. I've dealt with customers, spare parts, shortages, tools, engines, and gearboxes — including work on MAN TGA and F2000 trucks. I can disassemble and reassemble engines and gearboxes, and I've learned that understanding people can be just as important as understanding machines.

Where I'm Headed

One of my long-term goals is to build my own heavy-truck spare-parts import business — independently, using my father's experience and advice, but without depending on him financially. At the same time, I want to study Computer Science, ideally in the United States, and combine it with business, AI, and cybersecurity.

How I Work

I don't like blindly copying code or ideas. I want to understand why something works, where it belongs, and what's actually happening behind it. That's the same approach I bring to research — right now through the NYAS Junior Academy's BrainTech team, where my role is mainly programming and research.
